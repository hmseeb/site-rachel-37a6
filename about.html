Our Story

Started by Rachel, run like a neighbor should run it

Rachel founded Rapid Restore AZ after years of watching the same pattern play out in Valley homes: a pipe fails at midnight, the homeowner calls a national hotline, and a stranger shows up the next afternoon with equipment and no explanation. Weeks later the walls are still open and nobody returns the call.

We do it the other way around. When you call, you reach a person who knows the job — often Rachel herself. You get a straight answer about what we found, what it will cost, and how long it will take. And the same team that dries your home is the team that puts it back together.

We live here. Our crews drive the same freeways, sit out the same monsoons, and shop at the same stores you do. That's not a marketing line — it's why showing up matters to us.

Our Values

How we decide what to do

Restoration involves a lot of judgment calls. These are the rules we use when nobody's looking.

Urgency is the service

In restoration, speed is the quality. An hour saved on extraction can mean a wall that never has to come out. We treat every call like the clock is running, because it is.

Only do work that's needed

If a room doesn't need to be torn out, we don't tear it out. If you don't need us at all, we'll tell you that on the phone and it won't cost you a dime.

Document everything

Photos, moisture logs, itemized scopes. Good documentation protects you with your insurer and keeps us honest about what we actually did.

Treat the house like ours

Floor protection down, containment up, tools picked up at the end of every day. You're already dealing with enough without a messy job site.

Explain it in plain English

No jargon wall. We'll tell you what "Category 3 water" or "dry standard" means for your house, in words that actually help you decide.

Local accountability

We're not a franchise reading from a script in another state. If something goes wrong, you can reach the person whose name is on the business.

Our Process

The same four steps, every single time

Consistency is what makes an emergency manageable. Here's the sequence we follow on every job.

  1. Call & triage

    We get the critical facts, give you immediate safety steps, and dispatch the nearest available crew.

  2. Inspect & document

    Moisture meters, thermal imaging and photos. You get a written scope and a free estimate before we begin.

  3. Mitigate & monitor

    Extraction, containment and drying equipment, with documented daily readings until the structure is dry.

  4. Rebuild & review

    Repairs, finishes and a final walkthrough with you. We don't close a job you're not happy with.
