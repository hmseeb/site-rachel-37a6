Water Damage

Water Damage Restoration

A burst supply line can put hundreds of gallons into your floors before you're home from work. We extract standing water, pull saturated materials that can't be saved, and set commercial drying equipment sized to the actual moisture load — not a guess.

Because Arizona homes are largely slab-on-grade, water runs sideways under tile and into wall cavities. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to find every wet pocket, then document daily readings until the structure hits dry standard.

  • Common causes we handle: burst pipes, slab leaks, water heater failures, washer and dishwasher overflows, AC condensate line backups, refrigerator lines.
  • Equipment: truck-mounted extractors, high-velocity air movers, LGR dehumidifiers, HEPA air scrubbers.
  • Documentation: dated moisture logs and photos organized for your insurance claim.

Fire & Smoke

Fire & Smoke Damage Cleanup

Fire damage is rarely limited to what burned. Smoke moves through ductwork and settles into drywall, cabinets, upholstery and clothing, and acidic soot keeps etching surfaces long after the flames are out. Speed matters as much here as it does with water.

We stabilize the property, remove soot and char, clean and deodorize salvageable contents, and address the water damage the fire department inevitably leaves behind. Then we rebuild what was lost.

  • Soot & residue removal from walls, ceilings, ducts, hard surfaces and contents.
  • Odor elimination using HEPA filtration, thermal fogging and sealing where needed — not perfume-over-smoke.
  • Contents handling including on-site cleaning or pack-out of salvageable belongings.
  • Post-suppression water damage extracted and dried in the same mobilization.

Mold

Mold Remediation

People assume the dry desert means no mold. In reality, a slow leak under a sink or behind a shower wall creates a humid, dark cavity that grows mold beautifully — and you usually smell it before you see it.

Spraying bleach on the surface doesn't solve it. We contain the work area under negative air pressure so spores don't spread, remove affected porous materials, treat what remains, and most importantly, find and fix the moisture source that caused it.

  • Containment first — plastic barriers and negative air machines before anything is disturbed.
  • HEPA filtration running throughout the remediation.
  • Removal & treatment of affected drywall, insulation, baseboard and cabinetry.
  • Source correction so it doesn't come back three months later.

Signs you should call

  • A musty, earthy smell that won't go away
  • Dark spotting on drywall, grout or baseboards
  • Bubbling paint or warped trim near plumbing
  • Allergy-like symptoms that improve when you leave
  • A past leak that was never professionally dried
  • Soft or discolored wood under a sink

Storm

Storm & Monsoon Damage

Monsoon season turns a hairline roof crack into an indoor waterfall in about fifteen minutes. Add microburst winds, blown-in debris and haboob dust, and a single storm can leave a house wet, dirty and exposed all at once.

We respond with emergency tarping and board-up to stop further loss, then handle the interior drying, dust and debris cleanup, and the permanent repairs — so you're dealing with one team rather than four.

  • Emergency tarping and board-up to secure the structure immediately.
  • Roof leak, window and door intrusion extraction and structural drying.
  • Debris and dust cleanup after wind and haboob events.
  • Full repair of drywall, ceilings, insulation and finishes.

Biohazard

Sewage & Biohazard Cleanup

A sewer backup or toilet overflow is Category 3 water — it carries bacteria and pathogens, and it is not a mop-and-bucket situation. Porous materials it touches generally have to go.

Our crews work in full personal protective equipment, remove contaminated material, disinfect with hospital-grade products, dry the structure, and control odor so the space is genuinely safe for your family again.

  • Contaminated water extraction and safe disposal of affected porous materials.
  • Hospital-grade disinfection of all remaining surfaces.
  • Structural drying and deodorization after sanitizing.
  • Clear communication about what can be saved and what genuinely cannot.

Please don't do this yourself
