Our Process

What happens after you call

No mystery, no runaround. Here's exactly how a Rapid Restore AZ job runs from the first phone call to the final signature.

  1. You call, we answer

    A real person picks up — day or night. We walk you through immediate safety steps like shutting off the water or power while our crew rolls out.

  2. On-site assessment

    We inspect with moisture meters and thermal imaging, photograph everything for your claim, and give you a clear written scope and free estimate.

  3. Mitigate & dry

    Extraction, containment, air movers and dehumidifiers go in the same visit whenever possible. We monitor readings daily until the structure is dry.

  4. Restore & verify

    Repairs and finishes return the property to pre-loss condition. We close out with documentation your adjuster can accept and a walkthrough with you.
